About Us

DFZIP

Dingfu Zippers is a modern enterprise specializing in the research, development, production, and sales of zippers. Our main products include Spiral airtight zippers, Spiral Waterproof zippers, Plastic airtight zippers, YKK waterproof zippers, and airtight waterproof backpacks.

Equipped with advanced production facilities and a professional technical team, our products are widely used in outdoor gear, luggage, and apparel. Through exceptional quality and service, we have earned the trust and praise of numerous clients worldwide.

The zipper is one of the greatest inventions of the 20th century
Today Dingfu Technology reinvents the zipper

Dingfu Technology was founded in 2002 and later formally registered in 2016 to meet business development needs. Our registered trademark is "DFZIP". Committed to a market-oriented approach, innovation-driven development, and quality-based operations, we implement the "DFZIP" brand development strategy through a modernized management system. We focus on researching and manufacturing high-quality waterproof and airtight zippers that meet global customer demands. Since inception, we have prioritized brand building as our corporate foundation, pioneering brand strategy development in the industry. Our entire team is dedicated to establishing "DFZIP" as a leading trademark. We actively promote healthy industry development by participating in drafting national and international standards. Our products have obtained certifications from China, Europe, the United States, and other major markets worldwide.
