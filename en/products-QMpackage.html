Product Features

The DFZIP Spiral Airtight Zipper combines high-strength nylon tape with specialty TPU coating, engineered for extreme environments. Compared to conventional waterproof zippers, it delivers 30% higher tensile strength and 2× longer service life under sustained water pressure exposure.
