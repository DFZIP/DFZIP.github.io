Plastic Airtight Zipper

The DFZIP Plastic airtight zipper represents the pinnacle of industrial sealing technology, engineered specifically for high-pressure airtight, vacuum, and specialized protective applications. Its core features monolithically molded thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) teeth that eliminate traditional woven structures, combined with precision closure tolerances to create a seamless sealing barrier. Whether in positive-pressure inflation systems or vacuum environments, it achieves zero-leakage performance. Technical Specifications

Airtight Rating:ISO 17510 Class 2 (static airtight/vacuum)
Pressure Range:-1 Bar (vacuum) to +3 Bar (positive pressure)
Sealing Structure:Monolithic TPE teeth + airtight composite tape
Flex Life:≥10,000 cycles (-30°C to +90°C thermal cycling)
Chemical Resistance:Acid/alkali resistant, salt spray proof, UV stabilized (ISO 4892-3 compliant)
Material Composition:Specialty TPE teeth + high-strength Aramid fiber tape
